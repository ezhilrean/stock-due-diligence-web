Privacy Policy

1. About this policy

This policy describes the intended privacy practices of Stock Due Diligence, an informational tool for researching publicly listed companies.

2. Information and features

The site can be used to view public company research without an account. The website's source also includes optional account and saved-data features designed to use Supabase, including email sign-in, Google sign-in, a profile, watchlists, discounted-cash-flow assumptions, checklist progress, and saved research notes or reports.

Those account features are not available until Supabase is configured. If they are enabled, account details and saved content are processed by Supabase to provide the features. Do not enable those features or publish this policy until the deployed configuration has been checked and this description confirmed.

3. Public information and research

The product uses public company information and financial materials to support research. Do not submit sensitive personal information in research notes or reports.

4. Hosting and technical information

The website is hosted as a static site. Hosting, security, browser, and network infrastructure may process technical information such as request metadata or security logs. The specific providers, logs, cookies, analytics, retention periods, and any other processing must be verified against the live deployment before this policy is finalized.

5. Sharing and service providers

If Supabase authentication and storage are enabled, Supabase processes information needed to provide those features. The applicable provider terms and privacy documentation should be reviewed. No other sharing practices are asserted in this draft; verify all third-party services before publication.

6. Retention and deletion

When Supabase storage is enabled, saved records are associated with the user's account. The database schema is designed to cascade-delete related records when an authentication account is deleted, but the actual account-deletion workflow and provider-side retention must be verified before making a deletion promise. Hosting and provider logs may follow separate retention rules.

7. Security

The database design includes Row Level Security policies intended to restrict each signed-in user to their own records. Security depends on correct deployment and configuration; no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes

This policy must be updated when the product's data practices, authentication, analytics, or subscription/payment features change.

9. Contact

For support questions, use the Stock Due Diligence support page. A verified contact process must be maintained before this policy is published.
